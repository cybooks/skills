## Rules for the scorecard

- Every finding names an account with its balance, or a check code.
- Every finding is labelled Worked out or Read from the balances. One read
  from the balances is worded as a question: "Prepaid insurance did not
  move. Was the monthly release booked?"
- "Could not be checked" and "Not assessed" are never counted as Clear.
- A scored Cybooks check is worth a look at most. It is never a block and
  never "passed".
- Never compliant, clean, verified, audited or certified.
- Amounts in the books' currency, two decimals, in the number format of the
  person's own message (1,234.56 when they write in English); percentages
  to one decimal. No amount is "no amount"; a hidden one is "withheld".
- The same sections in the same order every run.
